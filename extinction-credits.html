Methodology

Version 2.3 · published

The price of a credit is a product of four legible factors and a reference unit. The intelligence multiplier is the moral horror of the instrument; it is stated openly so that it can be argued with.

PRICE = BASE × Sscarcity × Eendemism × Ffunction × Ccognition   (BASE = $18.00 reference unit)
FactorWhat it measuresSource basis
S — scarcityRarity and extinction risk, keyed to IUCN category and population estimate.IUCN Red List category (LC 1.0 → CR 8.0)
E — endemismGeographic irreplaceability; single-range and single-island taxa score higher.Range restriction (cosmopolitan 1.0 → island endemic 2.4)
F — ecological functionKeystone role, seed dispersal, nutrient cycling, ecosystem engineering.Functional-role literature (1.0 → 2.2)
C — cognition multiplierMeasured cognitive capacity: tool use, self-recognition, social cognition, problem solving.Comparative cognition literature (see below)

On the cognition multiplier. C is scaled from the comparative-cognition record, not from brain mass alone. The anchor findings, cited generally: mirror self-recognition in bottlenose dolphins (Reiss & Marino, PNAS, 2001) and in Asian elephants (Plotnik, de Waal & Reiss, PNAS, 2006); mark-test evidence in the Eurasian magpie (Prior, Schwarz & Güntürkün, PLoS Biology, 2008); tool manufacture in New Caledonian crows (Weir, Chappell & Kacelnik, Science, 2002); flexible tool use and culture in chimpanzees (Goodall; Whiten et al., Nature, 1999); referential communication in the grey parrot (Pepperberg, The Alex Studies, 1999); and technical problem solving in kea (Auersperg et al., 2011). Sperm whale and orca C values draw on the coda-dialect and cultural-transmission work of Whitehead & Rendell (The Cultural Lives of Whales and Dolphins, 2015). Any single-number cognition score is a reduction and we say so; the number exists to be disputed, not believed.

Invite to critique. The methodology is versioned and public. If a factor is wrong — a mis-keyed IUCN category, an overstated function score, a cognition claim that the literature does not support — write to the exchange and it will be corrected in the next version with the change logged. v2.3 supersedes v2.2 (Jul), which revised the endemism scale after external comment. Disagreement is the intended output of this instrument.

Terms

Stated prominently
1

Not a security. A credit is not a stock, bond, note, or investment contract, and is not offered as one.

2

Not an investment. There is no expectation of profit. It confers no financial interest of any kind.

3

Not tradeable. There is no secondary market. The order book on this page is a simulation.

4

No yield. A credit pays nothing, accrues nothing, and returns nothing.

5

Not resellable. A credit may not be assigned, transferred, or resold. It is registered to one holder.

NON-EXERCISABLE

A credit represents a right that cannot be used. It grants no permission, licence, authority, or property right over any organism, habitat, or land. No credit may be exercised — this appears on the certificate face and in every order confirmation. Taken as anything other than an artwork and a donation, it is void.

On the money. This is structured as a sale whose entire proceeds are donated onward to a named conservation partner (pending written consent), with published receipts. POLYGENIK does not keep the funds. If no conservation organisation will accept the money, this instrument has no floor and does not launch — that is a stated kill criterion, not a disclaimer. It is not an offset and makes no offset claim. Counsel review precedes any live sale; charitable-solicitation structure is handled through the partner or a fiscal sponsor.

The Novel

Source & credit
Venomous Lumpsucker
Ned Beauman
2022

This exchange descends directly from Ned Beauman's novel Venomous Lumpsucker (2022), which invents the World Commission on Extinction Emergencies and its extinction credits — a company that wipes out a species surrenders credits, and more intelligent species cost more.

The novel is satire. It lands because the underlying market already exists: England's Biodiversity Net Gain regime, decades of US wetland and species mitigation banking, and a wave of voluntary biodiversity-credit startups with real methodologies and real buyers. So this is not a parody of the book. It is the book's premise, built and priced and settled to conservation. The satire is that it functions.

With thanks and full credit to Ned Beauman. Read the novel.
