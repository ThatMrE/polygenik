The Collective
We build things that
shouldn't exist yet.

POLYGENIK is a collective of projects at the intersection of biology, technology, art, and community. We're not a company, not a studio, not a lab — we're the connective tissue between all of them.

Every project in the collective is born from the same conviction: that the most interesting things happen when you stop asking for permission and start building across boundaries. From biopunk fashion grown from mycelium to secret rooftop bars serving ultrasonic-aged cocktails, from living computational art to biological civil liberties advocacy — the thread that connects them all is a refusal to stay in one lane.
