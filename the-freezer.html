02THE ARGUMENTON CUSTODY

Who holds the key.

Custody is the thing biobanks have always taken without asking. The Freezer does not invent a new right. It gives you the boring paperwork that institutions kept for themselves.

01 · Scale The world's freezers are already full of people. A widely cited RAND analysis estimated more than 307 million tissue specimens stored in United States repositories by 1999, accumulating by roughly 20 million a year. Nobody has ever recounted them downward.

02 · The named collections UK Biobank alone holds on the order of 15 million aliquots drawn from 500,000 volunteers. National projects in the United States, China, Estonia and Iceland hold millions more. These are real, careful, well-run archives. That is exactly the point.

03 · Consent, in practice Consent at that scale is broad, one-time, and delegated. A signature at intake becomes a permission that outlives the memory of signing it. The sample stays; the person moves on. Very few donors could tell you their slot number, because they were never given one.

04 · The inversion The Freezer changes one variable. You get the number. You get the receipt. You can walk in and retrieve what is yours, and you can order it destroyed. The contents stay sealed; the register stays public. Custody, returned to the person it came from.

  • RAND, Handbook of Human Tissue Sources (1999): >307M specimens in U.S. repositories, +~20M/yr.
  • UK Biobank: ~500,000 participants; ~15M biological samples under −80 °C and vapour-phase storage.
  • Figures cited as published context for the argument; The Freezer is an independent facility and is not affiliated with any of the above.

05WHAT CANNOT GO INSCHEDULE A8 · PROHIBITED CONTENTS

The prohibited schedule.

Being visibly rigorous here is what makes everything above credible. If any of the following applies, we cannot accept your deposit, and we will say so plainly.

P-01 · SELECT AGENTS

✕ No select agents or toxins

Nothing on the U.S. Federal Select Agent list — no cultured pathogens, viable spores, or biological toxins. We are not a registered facility and never will be.

P-02 · CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES

✕ No controlled substances

Nothing scheduled under the Controlled Substances Act, and no unlabelled powders, pills, or extracts. A frozen slot is not a stash.

P-03 · HUMAN REMAINS

✕ No human remains or decedent tissue

California law on human remains is specific and we honour it. No tissue, fluid, or bone from a deceased person, and no ashes. Tissue and fluids are accepted only from a living, consenting donor.

P-04 · FETAL TISSUE

✕ No fetal or embryonic remains

No products of conception, fetal tissue, or embryonic material of any kind. This is a firm line, not a case-by-case one.

P-05 · CLINICAL SPECIMENS

✕ No unfixed clinical specimens

No fresh blood, unfixed biopsies, or diagnostic material shipped by post. Shipping instructions are issued only after an application is approved, and only for stable, non-infectious material.

P-06 · RESERVED RIGHTS

✕ We may refuse, and we may inspect

Sealed does not mean unexamined. We reserve the right to refuse any deposit, to inspect where law or safety requires, and to report where we are legally obliged to.

06TERMS & CUSTODYSLOT LICENCE A8 · SUMMARY

The four questions that make this an institution.

Answering these properly is the difference between an art project and a promise. The summaries below are governed by the full Slot Licence Agreement (A8), available on request before you deposit.

If Polygenik dissolves

Wind-down clause

A named successor custodian assumes the freezer under the same terms. If none can be found, depositors receive 180 days' written notice to retrieve, after which un-retrieved contents are destroyed on a stated timeline and logged. The freezer is never sold with contents inside.

If the freezer fails

Redundancy & honesty

Temperature is logged with SMS alerting; a backup CO₂/LN₂ plan and a documented response procedure are in place, with a second cabinet at a different address before slot 400 sells. We cannot guarantee against total loss, and the licence caps liability. We would rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.

If the term lapses

Your pre-chosen default

At deposit you choose one of two lapse behaviours: a $50 renewal reminder at year nine, or automatic transfer of the sealed vial into the public collection under a new anonymised slot number. No silent disposal, ever.

If the depositor dies

Named beneficiary

The one beneficiary named at deposit inherits full retrieval and destruction rights for the remainder of the term, on presentation of identity and the key tag or a certified copy of the death record. If no beneficiary is reachable, the term runs to its stated end and the lapse default applies.
