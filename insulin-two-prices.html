02 — The Sourcing

Where both numbers came from

03 — The Vials

Resin. No pharmaceutical content.

Both vials are solid casts of optically clear resin. They contain no insulin and no pharmaceutical product of any kind. The form is a generic vial: no manufacturer's label, brand or logo is reproduced anywhere on the object. The named insulin product appears only in the sourcing citation.

This is stated plainly because it matters both legally and honestly. The underside of every plinth is engraved with the same statement that appears on the certificate.

RESIN. NO PHARMACEUTICAL CONTENT.
